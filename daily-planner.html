Important: everything you do with this planner is processed locally in your browser.
If you refresh the page, everything will be lost. Export your progress using the 📅 or 📄 buttons.

🎯 Purpose of the Planner

Calculate how many tasks you can fit into a day and make changes to the schedule with a simple drag and drop.

🚀 Get Started

  1. Add task descriptions in TASK.
  2. Set the estimated time in DURATION.
  3. Fill in the top row of SCHEDULE — the planner auto-generates your schedule.

⚙️ Adjust Your Schedule

  • Change the first SCHEDULE cell to set a new starting time.
  • Drag rows to reorder or move tasks between days.
  • Update DURATION — your schedule updates instantly.
  • Lock fixed-time tasks (e.g. meetings) with the 🔒 checkbox — their SCHEDULE won't be changed.

🟢 Buttons Explained

  • ➕ — Add a row
  • 🗑️ — Delete the row (hover a row to see it)
  • 🗒️ — Add a new day
  • 📅 — Export as an .ics file (open it to add your schedule to Apple Calendar)
  • 📄 — Copy as text (paste into Notion or docs)

💾 Save & Resume Later

  1. Export with 📅.
  2. To restore, drag the downloaded .ics file onto the page — your schedule will reappear.

ℹ️ Notes

  • Green line "time gap" = free slot. Pops up and disappears automatically based on your schedule.
  • Red SCHEDULE cells = overlapping tasks
  • Mobile not supported — the planner is available only on desktop
